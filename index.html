What is Presence?

A practice, not a product.
A discipline, not a distraction.

Each session, reminders reach you at regular intervals — not to interrupt, but to ask: are you here? Over time, the intervals grow. The sessions lengthen. The reminders become less necessary.

Day by day, week by week, you discover an incredible shift in your day-to-day life. You can become consciously aware anywhere, doing anything — at work, at the gym, in conversation with someone you love. There is no excuse not to be here, right now, enjoying all of it.

Inside the App

Seven disciplines.
One unbroken thread.

⧖
Concentration Clock

Hold your attention on the second hand of a clock. One object, one point — the most ancient training tool made precise.

◈
Visualization

Place a form inside the mind and hold it. Custom images, multiple senses, all angles. The imagination becomes an instrument.

♩
Auditory

Listen with complete attention to a single sound until the sound and the listener are indistinguishable.

◎
Thought Control

Observe thoughts without following them. Hold a single thought to the exclusion of all others. Or achieve vacancy — true inner silence.

⬡
Asana

Sit without moving. Scratch nothing, adjust nothing. Let every impulse arise and pass. The body and the will are the same instrument.

✦
Soul Mirror

Track your genuine traits — positive and negative — with honesty. The mirror is the beginning of all real work.

How it works

Simple enough to start.
Deep enough to last a lifetime.

01
Choose a practice

Select from Awareness sessions, Concentration exercises, or Prayer. Each one targets a different layer of the mind.

02
Set your session

Choose your duration and reminder interval. Start with five minutes. Extend as your capacity grows. The app adapts to your progress.

03
Return when you drift

The app prompts you at intervals. Each prompt is a question: are you here? Each return strengthens the muscle of attention.

04
Track your growth

Sessions are logged with duration, quality scores, and notes. Watch your streak climb. Watch the intervals between reminders widen.
