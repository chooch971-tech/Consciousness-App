How it works
Star size and brightness reflect quality — sessions per tier, longest unbroken hold, post-session score. A Clock star after 3 years of practice is visibly larger and brighter than after 3 weeks.
Depth rings grow outward as total sessions accumulate. Each concentric ring = a tier milestone. At 10-min Clock mastery you might have 2 rings; at 15-min, 3.
Dim stars are available — no lock icon, no wall, just less light. Tapping them shows what Omnia says about them and how many sessions it would take to begin illuminating them.
The Kether gate at the crown glows gold when all Tiphareth-level stars reach a minimum brightness — it's not a lock, it's a lantern that lights up when you're ready for the next Bardon step.
Where it lives
A third tab inside Guide
Guide stays three tabs: Path (Omnia's daily agenda) · Upgrade (Omnia engine) · Tree (this chart).

Path drives the action. Tree reflects the result. The player moves between them naturally — they check Path to know what to do today, they open Tree to see what they've built.

Optionally: tapping Omnia in the banner crystal on the Path screen animates a transition into the Tree — the crystal "opens" into the star chart. This makes Omnia feel like the keeper of the map rather than a separate UI element.
