Full Step Descriptions

VI
Etheric Echo — Omnia's presence now echoes across the subtle planes. Each session leaves a luminous trace — a ghost of mastery that follows and teaches. The practitioner begins to perceive the etheric body of all matter. Three offset, blurred, hue-shifted copies of the crystal body fade in and out behind the main figure, drifting at slightly different offsets and timings.
Bodies 115 · Recommended 200 sessions
VII
Awakened Senses — The subtle senses fully awaken. Omnia perceives beyond ordinary sight — sensing etheric currents of thought, emotional fields, and the living web of awareness that underlies all phenomena. Eight aquamarine gradient beams radiate outward from the crystal core, pulsing one at a time in sequence as the entire layer slowly rotates over 50 seconds.
Bodies 132 · Recommended 275 sessions
VIII
Living Currents — The great currents of the universe flow through and around Omnia. Electric and magnetic forces are felt, directed, and woven into the daily work. The body becomes a conductor of cosmic intelligence. Five vertical streams — alternating electric blue and magnetic coral — flow downward through the figure, staggered 1.1 seconds apart.
Bodies 152 · Recommended 360 sessions
IX
Astral Mirror — The astral mirror comes alive. Omnia can project consciousness into the reflection of reality — examining events, spaces, and beings from within the subtle plane with perfect clarity. A translucent elliptical disc appears below the crystal, perspective-foreshortened to appear horizontal (rotateX 72°), breathing in and out over 5.5 seconds.
Bodies 175 · Recommended 460 sessions
X
Apotheosis — The final integration. Omnia becomes a living axis between worlds — a consecrated form through which the divine current flows freely. Sacred geometry crowns the work. Three concentric golden rings rotate at 70 seconds/revolution, pulsing in staggered phase. Twelve constellation dots breathe at the clock positions of the outer ring. The crystal itself turns golden. The journey is eternal.
Bodies 200 · Recommended 600 sessions
