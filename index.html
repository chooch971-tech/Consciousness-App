What is Presence?

An app for serious practitioners with measurable results.

Based on the teachings of some of the most enlightened spiritual masters, Presence makes their practices come to life, adapted to you. The difference between these exercises and others? You see measurable improvements day after day, week by week.

Following the teachings of Eckhart Tolle, the Awareness section of Presence is focused on placing your attention in the Now, an ability quintessential to growing spiritually. Each session, reminders reach you at regular intervals asking, are you here? Over time, the intervals will grow. The sessions will lengthen. The reminders become less necessary and you will see the present as it really is.

Following the teachings of Franz Bardon and Mouni Sadhu, these spiritual masters derived Concentration practices aged thousands of years to unlock incredible focus and imagination. This is how the ego fades, attention grows, and peace arrives. Armed with a section that reminds you to Pray daily, Presence contains everything you need for the ultimate spiritual progression.

How it works

Simple to start. Quick, drastic shifts in awareness.

01
