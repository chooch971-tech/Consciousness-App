How it works
Stars you've practiced glow bright. Depth rings show how deep your practice is. Constellation lines form between related exercises. Dim stars hint at what's coming. Hidden stars are barely visible until you're near them. Tapping any star shows your stats for that practice — no locks, no linear track.

Key insight: The star chart never tells you what to do next — Omnia's Path tab still does that. The chart only reflects what you've built. A new user sees one or two bright stars in a vast dark sky. A veteran sees constellations. Both experiences feel honest to the actual practice. The depth rings around practiced stars let experienced users see vertical progress even when the exercise name hasn't changed.
